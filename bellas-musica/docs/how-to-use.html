How to Use Bella's Fiestas

For families, vendors and the owner · Para familias, negocios y la dueña/el dueño

Bella's Fiestas is a website and phone app where families book everything for a party (music, food, tents, decorations, photographers and videographers, venues, dress and suit alterations) with real prices, open dates and a protected deposit. Everything works in English and Spanish: tap Menu → Español to switch.

Need help on any screen? Tap Don Chuy, the friendly helper in the corner. Ask him by typing or talking, in English or Spanish. He can find vendors and explain how things work. Tap Menu → Bigger text to make everything easier to read.

1. For families: book a vendor

Find

  1. Open the app. Pick what you need at the top: Music, Food, Rentals, Decorations, Photographers & videographers, Staff & services, Venues or Alterations.
  2. Type your ZIP code, and if you know them, the event, the date and the number of guests. Tap Search.
  3. Each card shows the starting price, how far the vendor is (📍 12 mi away), any travel fee, reviews and badges.

Compare

  1. Tap a vendor to see photos, videos, songs, packages, reviews and their cancellation policy.
  2. The green box says how far they are from you and whether they charge for travel.
  3. The calendar shows open days in gold. Days marked Booked are taken. Tap a gold day to see the open times.
  4. Tap the heart ♡ to save a vendor and compare later (Menu → Saved), or share it on WhatsApp.

Book

  1. Pick a day and a time, then a package. Fill in the guests, the party's ZIP, the address and any special requests.
  2. You see the full price, the deposit and the cancellation policy before you pay. You can do all of this before making an account.
  3. Tap to pay the deposit by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The time is held for you while you pay, so nobody else can take it.
  4. The vendor has 72 hours to accept. If they don't, your deposit comes back automatically.

After you book

  • Menu → My bookings: see each booking, message the vendor, pay the balance, ask for a new date, or cancel.
  • On the day, show the vendor your 4-digit arrival code; they enter it when they arrive.
  • If the vendor doesn't show up, report it within 3 days and you get back everything you paid in the app.
  • After the party, leave a review, and a tip if you'd like (the vendor gets all of it).

More for families

  • Get quotes (Menu): describe your party once, and up to 5 vendors who are free that day send you offers.
  • Plan a party (Menu): budget, all your vendors in one place, a guest list with RSVPs by WhatsApp, the court of honor, and a plan your family can see and vote on. Padrinos can pay their part.
  • Discover (Menu): short videos of local vendors.

2. For vendors: get booked

Make your page (about 10 minutes)

  1. Tap Menu → Sign up, then Menu → Groups and vendors → List your business.
  2. Add your name, type (mariachi, banda, DJ, catering, tents, dress alterations…), ZIP, prices or packages, photos and a video, the events you do, how far you travel, your deposit and your cancellation policy.
  3. Write in the language you prefer. The app shows your story and packages translated automatically to families reading in the other language, with your own words one tap away.
  4. Venues: add a virtual tour (a Matterport, Kuula, or 360° YouTube or Vimeo link) on the Photos tab so families can walk through your place.
  5. Open your free dates on the Calendar tab. Booked times close by themselves, so you're never double-booked.
  6. Set up payouts on the Payments tab (Stripe, about 5 minutes), then tap Publish.

Every day

  • Requests tab: every request comes with a paid deposit. Accept or decline within 72 hours. Quick replies are ready in English and Spanish.
  • A morning text lists today's parties and new requests (you can turn it off).
  • Messages tab: talk to families. Phone numbers are shared once a booking is confirmed.
  • On the day: ask the family for their arrival code and enter it to check in.

Getting paid

  • Your share of the deposit goes to your own Stripe account. The balance is paid in the app or directly to you.
  • Listing is free, and you never pay for leads. Bella's Fiestas keeps a 10% fee from the deposit (5% for founding vendors for 6 months, 6% with Pro). Tips have no fee.
  • For your first 3 events, your share is released after you check in at the party.

Grow

  • Share kit (Requests tab): your booking link, ready-to-post captions and a QR code for TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp and flyers.
  • My business tab: payment links for your own clients (3% fee), team logins for helpers, weekly gigs, your earnings.
  • Optional upgrades, each for 30 days: Featured ($49), Spotlight ($99), Pro ($29, lowers your fee to 6%).

3. For the owner: run the business

  • Menu → Admin: money, bookings, sign-ups, searches that found nobody (who to recruit next), the waitlist, chargebacks and no-show reports to decide.
  • Invite a business (Admin): copy the sign-up link, send it on WhatsApp, or print a flyer with a QR code.
  • Menu → Mi Oficina: your AI office. Six desks (Morning briefing, Recruiting, Social media, Vendor quality, Money & taxes, Support) write reports and drafts every morning. The Recruiting desk can also search the web for real local businesses to invite. Nothing goes out until you approve it. The Money & taxes desk gives a spreadsheet for your accountant.
  • Badges: mark vendors Verified or Insured after you check them; hide a listing if needed.
Install it like an app: on an iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Then turn on notifications under Account.

# Chicago launch kit

Goal: **10 to 15 real, published Chicagoland groups** and a first 5 bookings, before you spend a dollar promoting to customers. Supply first: a marketplace with no groups is worthless to a customer, but a customer request is the best thing you can show a group.

## The plan (4 weeks, alone, no calls)

| Week | Do | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Finish `docs/go-live.md`. Make a list of 40 groups (below). Send 15 messages/day (templates below). | Site live, 40 groups on your list, first replies |
| 2 | For each interested group, **create their listing yourself** (Admin, Invite a group) and send the private link. They only tap "Claim", add photos, and Publish. | 10 invited, 5 published |
| 3 | Push for photos and a video link on each profile (best predictor of bookings). Verify each real group (see below). Post the Chicago page in local Facebook groups and WhatsApp. | 10 published |
| 4 | Set `DEMO_SEED=0` once you have 10+ real groups. Start customer outreach (Chicago page: quinceañera and wedding groups, community pages, venues). | first 5 bookings |

## Finding groups (all free, all from your desk)

- Search Instagram and Facebook: "mariachi Chicago", "banda Chicago", "norteño Little Village", "mariachi Cicero", "grupo musical Pilsen", "trío romántico Chicago", plus suburbs (Berwyn, Melrose Park, Aurora, Elgin, Joliet, Waukegan).
- YouTube and TikTok "mariachi Chicago" results; a group with recent posts is active.
- Chicago Mariachi/Latin music groups on Facebook, Latino wedding vendor pages, quinceañera planners' "vendor" lists, and venue vendor lists (banquet halls list their musicians).
- Aim for variety: 4 mariachi, 3 banda or norteño, 2 grupera/conjunto, 2 trío, 1-2 DJ. Prefer **active** groups (posted this month) with **video**.

Keep a simple sheet: group, link, type, date contacted, replied, invited, claimed, published, verified.

## Message templates

Send from a **business Instagram/Facebook page** or a business number. Keep it short; a link to their own listing is the whole pitch. Send the first message in Spanish unless their page is in English.

**Spanish, first message**
> Hola, soy [Tu nombre] de Bella's Música. Estamos lanzando una página para que familias en Chicago reserven mariachis, bandas y grupos norteños para quinceañeras, bodas y fiestas. Vi su trabajo y me encantaría tenerlos. Es gratis publicar; solo cobramos una comisión pequeña cuando reciben una reserva pagada, y el cliente paga un depósito por adelantado, así que se acaban los "no llegó". ¿Les mando el enlace de cómo se vería su página? Sin compromiso.

**English, first message**
> Hi, I'm [Name] with Bella's Música. We're launching a site where Chicago families book mariachi, banda and norteño groups for quinceañeras, weddings and parties. I saw your work and would love to have you. Listing is free; we only take a small fee on paid bookings, and customers pay a deposit up front so there are fewer no-shows. Can I send you a link to what your page would look like? No commitment.

**Follow-up after 3 days (no reply)**
> Solo quería dar seguimiento. Ya tenemos [N] solicitudes de familias en [zona] esta semana. ¿Les puedo mandar el enlace? / Just following up; we're getting requests from families in [area]. Can I send the link?

*(Only say "requests" once it's true. Don't invent numbers.)*

**When they say yes:** ask for 3 things in one message: **name and ZIP of where you're based**, **price per hour and what you play** (songs, events), **3-5 photos and a video link** (a WhatsApp/Instagram video link is fine to start). Then create the draft in **Admin, Invite a group**, and reply:

> ¡Gracias! Aquí está su página lista: [enlace privado]. Abran el enlace, creen su cuenta y toquen "Reclamar". Después solo agreguen sus fechas libres y toquen "Publicar". Les llega un aviso por correo cuando alguien pida una fecha.
> / Here's your page, ready: [private link]. Open it, create your account, tap "Claim", add your open dates and tap "Publish". You'll get an email when someone requests a date.

The link works once and only for the first person who signs in with it. If they lose it, use **New link** on the Admin page.

## Verified and Insured badges

Give **Verified** only when you have confirmed the group is real and working: an active social page with recent events, plus a video or reference you can see. Give **Insured** only when you have seen proof of liability insurance (many banquet halls require it, so groups often have it and will happily send a photo). Admin, Groups, the Mark verified / Mark insured buttons. Every change is logged.

## Getting the first customers

- Post the Chicago page (`YOUR-DOMAIN/c/chicago`) in neighborhood Facebook groups (Pilsen, Little Village, Cicero, Berwyn...), community and parish pages, and quinceañera/wedding planning groups. Share **a real group's page** (`/g/...`), not the homepage: it shows a photo card in WhatsApp/iMessage.
- Ask each published group to share **their own** link. Their followers are the best first customers.
- Ask banquet halls and photographers to list you as a preferred way to find music.
- Tell early customers you're new: a few honest reviews beat a big launch.

## What to say when a group asks "why you and not GigSalad / Thumbtack?"

(Details and sources in `docs/competitors.md`.) Keep it to these, and only say what the site really does:
- **You never pay for leads.** A customer has to put down a deposit before you even hear from them, so no fake or "just browsing" requests. You pay a small fee only on paid bookings.
- **Your price and open dates are on your page.** Families book without waiting for a quote.
- **Discover feed:** post a short vertical clip (a YouTube Short) and it plays for families scrolling near you. Free to be in it; paying for Featured makes you show up more often, always labelled Promoted.
- **Protection both ways:** the customer's deposit is theirs to lose if they cancel late per your policy; if you're not able to show up, they are refunded. The arrival code on the day proves you were there.
- **Spanish and English**, made for quinceañeras, bodas and serenatas.

## Rules of thumb

- **Response time is the product.** A request that sits three days dies. If a group is slow, message them yourself.
- Don't promise what the site doesn't do. It doesn't guarantee a group will show up; it collects a deposit, applies a stated cancellation policy, and keeps everything in writing.
- Never share a customer's phone before the group accepts (the site already enforces this).
- If a group cancels late, refund the customer in full (the site does), and remove the group's Verified badge if it repeats.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone (optional), language, and a securely hashed password.
  • Booking details: event type, date, place, guest count, your messages to groups, and the amount and status of payments and refunds.
  • For groups: listing details, photos, video links, calendar, and the phone number you give for text alerts.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address, used for security and to limit abuse.

What we do not collect

We never see or store card numbers. Payments are handled by Stripe.

How we use it

To run the marketplace: show your booking to the group, process deposits and refunds, send booking notifications, prevent fraud, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Who sees your information

  • The group you book sees your name, event details and messages. Your phone number is shared with them only after they confirm your booking.
  • Stripe (payments and payouts to groups), Twilio (text messages), and our hosting provider [HOST] process data for us.
  • OpenStreetMap serves map tiles, and YouTube or Vimeo serve videos that groups embed; those sites may receive your IP address when you view a map or video.

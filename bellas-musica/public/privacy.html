Last updated: October 19, 2026 (version 2026-10-19). [YOUR BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS] ("we", "us") runs Bella's Fiestas. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, and your choices. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party ads.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, password (stored only as a secure scrambled "hash"), phone (optional), language, whether you agreed to texts, and the version of these terms you agreed to and when.
  • Bookings and parties: event type, date, time, place, guest count, notes, what the vendor needs from you, messages, signed agreements, reviews, saved vendors, and your party plan (budget, timeline, votes and comments from family you share it with).
  • Payments: amounts, status, refunds and payout status. Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Guest lists: if you plan a party, the names and phone numbers of guests you add and their RSVP answers.
  • Vendors: business and listing details, photos and videos, calendar, prices, team helpers, payout status from Stripe (not bank numbers), and license or insurance documents you upload. Those documents are kept private: they are not on your public page and are seen only by us, to check them.
  • Waitlist: email and ZIP code if you ask to hear when we open in your area.
  • Location: if you tap "Use my location", your browser sends us your approximate position once, only to find your ZIP code. We don't keep it.
  • Technical: your IP address, used to keep accounts safe and to limit abuse (for example, too many login attempts). For reviews from a vendor's past clients we keep only a scrambled form of the IP address, to stop vendors from reviewing themselves. If the app hits an error on your device, the error details (not your personal data) are sent to us so we can fix it.
  • Usage counts: we count things like searches, page views and bookings, by area and by listing, to show vendors how they are doing and to improve the Service. These counts are kept on our own server and not shared with ad networks.

Cookies and storage on your device

We use one cookie, to keep you logged in. Your browser also remembers a few settings on your device: your language, "Bigger text", whether you installed the app, when to ask again about notifications, the partner link you came from (if any), and whether Don Chuy reads answers aloud. Your conversation with Don Chuy is kept only in that browser tab. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show your request to the vendor, take deposits and payments, pay vendors, give refunds, and send booking emails, texts and notifications.
  • To keep everyone safe: prevent fraud and fake reviews, handle no-show reports and chargebacks, and enforce our Terms.
  • To improve the Service and to show vendors their own numbers.
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax and payment records.

Don Chuy, the AI helper

Don Chuy is an optional helper powered by artificial intelligence (Claude, made by Anthropic). Nothing is sent to him unless you open him and ask something.

  • What is sent to Anthropic to write an answer: your question and the conversation so far, your first name if you are logged in, the screen's language, whether Bigger text is on, the screen you are on, and what he looks up for you: public listings and, if you ask about them, your own bookings (vendor, date, status and amounts). We never send your email, phone number, address, card details, private notes or arrival codes.
  • What we keep: we do not store your conversations with Don Chuy on our servers. The conversation stays in your browser tab and is gone when you close it or tap "New conversation". We keep only daily totals (how many questions, and what they cost) to stay within our budget.
  • Anthropic: processes the conversation to answer it, under its commercial terms. [Anthropic does not use these conversations to train its models, and keeps them for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.]
  • Talking and listening: if you tap the microphone, your device's own speech service (for example Apple's or Google's) turns your voice into text; we receive only the text. Reading answers aloud uses your device's own voice.
  • Don't share card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information with Don Chuy.

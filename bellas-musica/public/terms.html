3. How booking works

  • Prices. Vendors set their own prices, packages, add-ons, minimum hours, travel fees, deposit percentage and cancellation policy. The total, the deposit and the cancellation policy are shown before you pay and are saved with your booking. Prices are in U.S. dollars. Customers pay no booking fee to us.
  • Requesting. You request a date and time and pay the deposit. While you are paying, the time is held for 30 minutes.
  • The vendor answers. The vendor has 72 hours to accept (less if the event is sooner). If the vendor declines or doesn't answer in time, the request is cancelled and your deposit is refunded in full automatically.
  • Quotes and custom offers. With "Get quotes", your request goes to up to 5 vendors that are free that day. Vendors can send you a custom offer with their own price. An offer becomes a booking only when you accept and pay it.
  • Weekly bookings. Some vendors (for example at restaurants) can be booked for the same time every week, up to 12 weeks, in one payment.
  • Changing the date. You can ask a vendor to move a booking. The new time is held for the vendor for 3 days. If the vendor doesn't accept, the booking stays as it was.
  • Agreement. Some bookings include an agreement that the customer and vendor sign in the app. Typing your name and tapping to sign is an electronic signature and is as binding as signing on paper.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before the vendor accepts, you can cancel for a full refund.
  • After the vendor accepts, your refund depends on the vendor's policy shown at checkout and on how many days remain before the event:
    • Flexible: full deposit back 3 or more days before.
    • Moderate: full deposit back 7 or more days before; 50% from 3 to 6 days before.
    • Strict: full deposit back 30 or more days before; 50% from 14 to 29 days before.
    No refund is due inside those windows. Under every policy, the deposit is not refunded for a last-minute cancellation (the last 3 calendar days before the event, which covers the final 48 hours).
  • If the vendor cancels a confirmed booking, you get back everything you paid through the Service.
  • Weather. For outdoor events, the vendor's weather policy shown on its page applies, in addition to the rules above.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show them.
  • If you open a chargeback with your bank instead of contacting us, we may pause your account while it is reviewed.

6. The Show-up guarantee

  • Each confirmed booking has a 4-digit arrival code. The customer shows it on the day, and the vendor enters it to check in.
  • If the vendor never checked in and did not show up, the customer can report it within 3 days after the event. We look at both sides. If we find the vendor did not show up, we refund everything the customer paid through the Service and take the vendor's share back.
  • For a vendor's first 3 events on Bella's Fiestas, its share of the deposit is held until it checks in at the party, or until 4 days after the event if no problem was reported.
  • Our decision covers only money paid through the Service. It does not stop either side from using other legal remedies against the other.
